\subsection{Gate Definitions}

The default offline gate evaluator requests at least \(256\) observations.
Gate O checks: a positive 95\% confidence interval for wrong and shuffled loss
gaps; relative gaps of at least \(5\%\) and \(2\%\), respectively; no mean
oracle degradation from base; at least four active options with at least
\(1\%\) share; effective option number at least \(3\); dominant share at most
\(0.8\); median applied residual at least \(10^{-4}\); and 95th-percentile
residual ratio at most \(0.25\).  When event IDs are available, option--event
NMI must exceed a shuffled-code baseline by at least \(0.02\).  Gate R
additionally checks router accuracy at least \(0.35\), at least four routed
options with at least \(1\%\) share, effective option number at least \(3\),
dominant share at most \(0.8\), router flow degradation from base at most
\(2\%\), and router-oracle flow gap at most \(10\%\).  These are initial
engineering thresholds, not universal scientific constants; they must be
frozen before checkpoint selection.

\subsection{Training and Inference Pseudocode}

\paragraph{U1 oracle step.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Encode images/instruction with Qwen3-VL and native state with
    \(E_s^e\).
    \item Encode the demonstrated native action chunk with \(E_a^e\) at
    \(\tau=0\); summarize masked mean, variation, endpoint displacement, and
    peak adjacent change; infer and quantize the posterior option.
    \item If event balancing is enabled, classify the native chunk by temporal
    change strata and compute checkpointed inverse-frequency discovery weights.
    \item Sample one \(\tau,\epsilon\), then compute fusion-off and
    correct-option flow losses with the same pair.
    \item At the configured optimizer cadence, sample a guaranteed-different
    code and compute the wrong-option flow loss using the same pair.
    \item Optimize flow, weighted VQ/commitment, entropy-floor usage,
    separation, weighted motion, counterfactual, and improve losses; keep the
    router frozen.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{U2 router step.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Recompute the frozen posterior label and current-context state
    feature without gradient.
    \item Optimize router cross-entropy only, using posterior-code
    inverse-frequency weights when router balancing is enabled.
    \item At the diagnostic cadence, pass the hard routed code through the
    frozen action expert and compare its flow loss with base and oracle.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Inference query.}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Encode current visual/language/state inputs; do not provide future
    actions.
    \item Compute hard router logits and update the controller for the supplied
    environment/episode IDs.
    \item Embed the active code, create its option token and FiLM residual, and
    integrate the shared flow expert.
    \item Decode the normalized native action chunk and record it in the
    corresponding controller stream.
\end{enumerate}
